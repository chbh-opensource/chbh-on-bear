# Procedure to request Holiday Cover

### **Procedure to request Holiday Cover and what it means for the volunteer/s**

1. MEG Support emails the dates of his annual leave (AL) to the MEG Operators (*ideally sent a few weeks before AL is due to start*), 
asking for Holiday Cover volunteers, with a link to GitHub detailing the remunerations available and probable solutions to follow if there are any issues while he is away.

2. Once a MEG Operator has volunteered (*ideally two MEG Operators - to help each other out as/if necessary*), a slot, or slots, is/are booked 
on Calpendo to go over the procedures/solutions (*who to telephone/the use of Remote Desktop Access, etc*) or any questions posed by the volunteer/s.

3. Once MEG Support has returned from AL and **is happy that volunteers have performed HC duties as expected**, CHBH Manager will arrange £32 in Amazon vouchers 
to be given to the volunteers/s, or ? unfunded hours if requested instead.

	- If any overnight call out has required CHBH on-site attendance, rather than just remote assistance from home, then the relevant vouchers, or ? hours, 
will also be given/transferred over (*£16 Amazon vouchers, or ? hours, for every on-site visit*), again after MEG Support has returned from AL **and is happy the cover was provided correctly**.
