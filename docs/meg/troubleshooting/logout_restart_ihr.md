# To Logout or Restart the IHR

### **Logout**

Via the IHR PC directly, in room G24 - **<span style="color:red">don't attempt remotely!</span>**

- **Logout of the IHR** (upper right corner of the screen, labelled **“Desktop”**), then log back in with the **megadmin** account (p/wd: **[Information]**).
	- **```“Recent Activity” -> “Leave” -> “Logout”```**

### **Restart**

- Put the IHR into “**Maintenance**” mode, then restart from the same place as the “**Logout**” option above.
	- **```“Recent Activity” -> “Leave” -> “Restart Computer”```**

Once rebooted, **check IHR** is back in **Normal** mode. If necessary, click on the **Normal** button. 

- Check by **examining the LEDs** on the **IHR cabinet door**.
